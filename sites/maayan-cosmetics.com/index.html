About the Founder

Meet Maayan Vaknin

Maayan is a certified clinical cosmetologist specialising in advanced, highly customised, and results-oriented skincare. Her mission is simple: to empower every client to feel confident, revitalised, and radiant in their own skin.

Her methodology combines cutting-edge skincare technologies, high-grade materials, and thorough personal follow-ups — treating acne, seborrhea, anti-aging, and deep skin rehabilitation with equal precision and care.

The clinic is a seamless blend of top-tier medical professionalism, goal-driven results, and a warm, home-like environment where you always feel at ease.
