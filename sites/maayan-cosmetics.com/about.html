Meet Dr. Juliah Tbarani

Beauty is a right, not a privilege

We believe beauty is for everyone. We have made non-surgical beauty treatments affordable and effective, achieving natural-looking results with the minimum of fuss. When we feel beautiful we feel healthier, happier, and more confident.

That is why LIKHA Aesthetic Clinic is fully certified, using traceable, high-quality products. All our treatments are only ever overseen by Dr Juliah Tbarani, who is a fully trained, award-winning and experienced Medical & Aesthetic Doctor.

As an award winning doctor with 21+ years experience working in medical, surgical and general practice fields, patient care is always her foremost concern. As a mother, Dr Juliah understands the pressure of balancing home and professional life, whilst trying to look your best.
